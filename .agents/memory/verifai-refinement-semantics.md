---
name: VerifAI refinement meaning
description: Product semantics for idea refinement in VerifAI.
---

For VerifAI, idea refinement means AI-generated suggestions that improve how a founder frames, tests, and sharpens the idea. It does not mean asking the founder to edit or refine their submitted answers.

**Why:** the user explicitly distinguished AI coaching from answer editing.

**How to apply:** keep answer review/editing separate from the AI refinement recommendations in reports and coaching surfaces.
